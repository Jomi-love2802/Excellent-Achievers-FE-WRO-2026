# EVA TESLA
EVA TESLA is our autonomous self-driving robot developed for the World Robot Olympiad (WRO) Future Engineers 2026 competition. The robot is designed to demonstrate how an autonomous vehicle can operate without human intervention by combining intelligent programming, precise engineering, and sensor-based navigation.

The name EVA represents innovation, intelligence, adaptability, and the ability to respond to changing situations, while TESLA represents electrical engineering, automation, technological advancement, and smart mobility. Together, the name EVA TESLA represents our vision of creating a reliable and intelligent autonomous robot capable of making real-time decisions in different environments.

EVA TESLA was built with a strong yet lightweight structure to provide stability while maintaining efficient movement. It features a realistic steering mechanism, an optimized chassis, and integrated sensors that allow the robot to detect and respond to its surroundings.

Throughout the development process, we followed a systematic engineering approach that involved research, design, prototyping, testing, evaluation, and continuous improvement. Each stage helped us identify problems, improve the robot’s performance, and achieve greater accuracy, stability, reliability, and consistency.

EVA TESLA is more than just a competition robot. It is a practical demonstration of robotics, autonomous systems, embedded programming, electronics, and engineering innovation. Working on the project has helped us develop important skills in mechanical design, electronics, programming, problem-solving, teamwork, and critical thinking.

Through EVA TESLA, we aim to demonstrate how intelligent autonomous technology can contribute to safer, more efficient, and smarter transportation systems. Most importantly, the project reflects our creativity, technical knowledge, teamwork, and commitment to continuous improvement, which are essential qualities of the WRO Future Engineers competition.

The final programming aims to ensure that the robot can complete the WRO Future Engineers challenge with a high level of precision, stability, reliability, and adaptability.

# CLOCKWISE PROGRAMMING 
The clockwise program enables EVA TESLA to navigate the WRO Future Engineers track autonomously in a clockwise direction using the LEGO EV3 controller, motors, and sensors.

When the program starts, the robot first initializes and calibrates its motors and sensors. This helps ensure that the sensor readings are accurate and that the robot is ready to move correctly. The drive motor moves the robot forward, while the steering motor controls the front wheels, allowing EVA TESLA to make smooth and controlled turns.

As EVA TESLA moves around the track, it continuously collects information from its sensors. This information helps the robot maintain its position within the lane and recognize corners and obstacles. The program processes the sensor data in real time and adjusts the steering angle and speed according to the conditions of the track.

On straight sections of the track, the robot maintains a steady speed to improve efficiency. When approaching a bend, the program automatically reduces the speed and adjusts the steering to help the robot make the turn smoothly and accurately.

The clockwise program works through a continuous sense–analyze–decide–act process. The robot constantly monitors its surroundings, analyzes the information received from its sensors, determines the appropriate action, and sends commands to the steering and drive motors.

When an obstacle is detected, the program changes the robot’s movement so that it can safely navigate around the obstacle before returning to its normal path. This allows EVA TESLA to respond to changes on the track without human intervention.

The program was improved through extensive testing, debugging, and fine-tuning. Steering angles, motor speeds, sensor thresholds, and turning parameters were adjusted repeatedly to improve accuracy, reduce errors, and achieve more consistent lap completion.

Through this process, the clockwise program enables EVA TESLA to drive smoothly, accurately, and autonomously, while adapting to different track conditions and maintaining reliable performance.

# ANTI-CLOCKWISE PROGRAMMING 
The anti-clockwise program enables EVA TESLA to navigate the WRO Future Engineers track autonomously in a counter-clockwise direction. The program is designed to help the robot move smoothly around the track while responding to lane boundaries, corners, and obstacles.

Before the robot begins moving, the program initializes the motors and calibrates the sensors. This ensures that the sensors provide accurate readings and that the robot starts its run in a stable and controlled manner.

As EVA TESLA travels around the track, it continuously collects information from its distance and colour sensors. The sensor data is processed in real time to determine the robot’s position, detect obstacles, and monitor the lane boundaries. Based on this information, the EV3 Brick controls the steering motor to keep the robot on the correct driving line while regulating the drive motor for smooth and controlled movement.

When EVA TESLA detects an obstacle in its path, the program determines a suitable route around it. The robot temporarily reduces its speed and adjusts the steering angle to improve control and manoeuvrability. After passing the obstacle safely, the robot gradually returns to its original path and continues navigating the track.

Throughout the anti-clockwise run, the program operates through a continuous cycle of sensing, processing, decision-making, and movement. This allows EVA TESLA to respond quickly to changes on the track, maintain stability while turning, reduce steering errors, and complete the required laps without human intervention.

The anti-clockwise program was improved through repeated testing, debugging, and adjustment. Different steering angles, speeds, sensor settings, and obstacle-avoidance responses were tested to improve turning accuracy, stability, and overall performance.

Through this development process, EVA TESLA became more consistent and reliable when navigating the track in the anti-clockwise direction. The program is designed to provide smooth movement, accurate steering, effective obstacle avoidance, and dependable autonomous performance throughout the WRO Future Engineers challenge.
